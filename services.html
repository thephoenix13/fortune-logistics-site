Service 01

Full Truck Load (FTL)

A dedicated vehicle, a direct route, and a single client's cargo — that's FTL. Ideal for high-volume shipments where speed, safety, and payload efficiency matter most. No consolidation. No waiting. Your truck, your timeline.

  • Dedicated vehicle — no co-loading
  • Direct origin-to-destination routing
  • Available in 10T, 20T, 32T, 40T configurations
  • GPS tracked, Digital POD on delivery

Service 02

Part Truck Load (PTL)

Pay only for the space your cargo needs. PTL consolidates multiple clients' shipments on a single vehicle — giving you the economics of shared freight without sacrificing tracking or delivery commitments.

  • Consolidated freight — cost-efficient
  • Full end-to-end tracking on every consignment
  • Regular departure schedules on key lanes
  • Digital POD and WhatsApp milestone updates

Service 03

ODC / Project Cargo

Over-dimensional cargo requires more than a bigger truck. It requires specialist knowledge, route surveys, police escort coordination, and specialized trailers built for abnormal loads. Fortune Logistics manages the entire move — from route planning to delivery confirmation.

  • Specialized trailers: flatbed, lowbed, extendable
  • Pre-move route survey and permit acquisition
  • Police escort arrangements where required
  • Project coordination from loading to delivery

Packaging Solutions

Transit damage starts before the truck moves. Fortune Logistics provides industrial-grade packaging engineered for road freight — from standard wooden crating to custom foam-lined solutions for precision equipment.

  • Wooden crating for heavy industrial equipment
  • Foam-lined custom packaging for sensitive cargo
  • Pallet wrapping and strapping to transport standards
  • On-site packaging at origin, unpacking at destination
