Who We Serve

Industrial Freight Is Not Standard Logistics.

Over-dimensional cargo doesn't behave like standard freight. A flatbed carrying a 40-tonne transformer or a lowbed moving a mining excavator needs a completely different approach — from the moment the load is measured to the final handoff on site.

Fortune Logistics has built a specialist industrial freight capability: a fleet of flatbed, lowbed, and extendable trailers; in-house route survey competency; permit acquisition across state RTOs; and a dedicated project coordinator for every industrial move. We've handled infrastructure, energy, manufacturing, and plant-relocation freight across Maharashtra and beyond.

Challenges We Solve

What Makes Industrial Freight Hard — And How We Handle It.

Route Complexity
ODC loads can't just take the highway. We survey the route, identify low bridges, narrow roads, and weight restrictions — and plan the optimal path before the truck moves.
Permits & Escorts
State permits, police NOC, district collector clearances, escort vehicles — we manage every approval and coordination so your project timeline isn't held up by paperwork.
Specialized Equipment
Not every ODC load is the same. We deploy the right trailer — flatbed for wide loads, lowbed for tall machinery, extendable for ultra-long cargo — matched precisely to your requirement.
