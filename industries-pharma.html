Who We Serve

Pharma Freight Needs More Than a Truck.

The pharmaceutical sector operates under some of the strictest logistics requirements in India. GDP (Good Distribution Practice) guidelines demand documented pickups, chain-of-custody handoffs, and verifiable proof of delivery — not as a preference, but as a regulatory requirement.

Fortune Logistics works with pharma clients who need a logistics partner that understands these standards without needing them explained from scratch. Our processes are built around documentation, traceability, and cold-chain awareness — so your cargo moves in compliance with your regulatory obligations.

Services We Provide

The Right Service for Your Pharma Freight.

Full Truck Load (FTL)
Dedicated vehicles for pharma-only cargo — no co-loading, direct routing, GPS tracked throughout. Ideal for bulk pharma distribution runs.
Part Truck Load (PTL)
Consolidated pharma lanes with per-consignment tracking. Cost-efficient for smaller volumes without sacrificing compliance or traceability.
Packaging Solutions
Tamper-evident, temperature-stable packaging for pharma in transit. Custom solutions for vials, blister packs, bulk API, and medical devices.
ODC / Project Cargo
Medical equipment, lab machinery, and large pharmaceutical plant installations — moved with specialist trailers and full documentation.
