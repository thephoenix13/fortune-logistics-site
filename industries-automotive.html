India's Automotive Supply Chain Runs on Precision.

Automotive manufacturing is built on JIT — Just-in-Time. When parts don't arrive on schedule, assembly lines stop. Fortune Logistics operates dedicated automotive freight lanes across the Pune-Nashik-Chakan-Mumbai corridor, with dispatches timed to your production schedule, not our convenience.

We serve Tier-1 and Tier-2 automotive suppliers moving components, sub-assemblies, and finished vehicles. Our drivers are briefed on automotive cargo handling protocols — proper securing, component protection, and handling procedures that prevent damage in transit. Every shipment is GPS-tracked, and Digital POD is issued at delivery.
