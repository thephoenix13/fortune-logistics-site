Our Story

A Logistics Company
Built Different.

Fortune Logistics was founded in 2022 in Pune with a clear conviction: India's road freight sector was broken because too many transporters were just brokers in disguise — middlemen who didn't own a single truck, couldn't guarantee delivery timelines, and had no accountability when things went wrong.

We took a different route. From day one, every vehicle in our fleet is owned and operated by us. No sub-contracting, no hand-offs, no excuses. That ownership gives us complete control over vehicle condition, driver conduct, loading practices, and delivery timelines.

"We built Fortune Logistics because we were tired of logistics companies that didn't own their trucks."

In 2023, we earned our ISO 9001-2015 certification — formalising the quality management processes we'd already been running. By 2024, we'd expanded to 50+ cities across all 28 states. Today, 500+ active clients — from automotive giants to pharmaceutical manufacturers — trust us to move their most critical freight.
