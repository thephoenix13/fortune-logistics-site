Owned vs Brokered

Ownership Changes Everything.

Most logistics companies don't own their trucks. They broker freight to third-party operators — which means the truck that shows up might not be GPS-enabled, the driver might not be trained to your standards, and the service quality is unpredictable.

Fortune Logistics is different. Every vehicle in our fleet is owned outright. Every driver is trained and vetted by us. Every truck carries GPS. There is no broker in between — just direct accountability from dispatch to delivery.

When we say it's our truck, it actually is. That's where our accountability starts.

ODC & Project Cargo

Specialized Trailers for Non-Standard Loads.

Over-dimensional cargo needs more than a bigger truck. Our specialist trailer fleet handles what standard vehicles can't.

Flatbed Trailer
Open-deck platform for oversized cargo — heavy machinery, steel structures, construction materials, and industrial equipment that can't fit in a closed body.
Lowbed / Well Bed
Ultra-low deck height for extra-tall cargo — power transformers, heavy plant equipment, and oversized machinery requiring minimum vertical clearance.
Extendable Trailer
Adjustable length configuration for elongated cargo — steel pipes, I-beams, girders, wind turbine components, and long structural elements.
